## The word

**Unsubscribe**

One word, eleven characters. The ex, summed up as spam you finally opted
out of — not a plain insult, but a verdict anyone who has had an ex
recognises at a glance: the relationship was unsolicited mail, and you hit
unsubscribe.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the single word to `/output/entry.txt` (exactly one word,
no punctuation, no extra text).

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
